About Tamagotchi

Tamagotchi is a small virtual pet that needs care and attention. Players can feed their Tamagotchi, play with it and help it grow into different characters.

How to Take Care of a Tamagotchi

Daily Activities

  • Feed your Tamagotchi when it is hungry.
  • Play games to keep it happy.
  • Let it rest when it is tired.
  • Keep its environment clean.
